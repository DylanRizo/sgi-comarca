# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Four authenticated operators manage inventory across three configured warehouses, on desktop and mobile browsers. Inventory operations are shared; financial access and valuation are separately authorized.

## Product Purpose

SGI La Comarca supports finding products, receiving merchandise, transferring stock, selling and physically counting inventory, with clear feedback and an auditable history.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Spanish interface, NIO currency and America/Managua business dates. Product creation may include an atomic first receipt. Open counts support audited corrections; submitted counts are locked. Unknown costs remain unknown. Historic movements and valuations are immutable. The user chooses light or dark appearance per account and browser. Online operation only.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the established green identity, familiar controls and compact operational layouts. Actions must be discoverable on the relevant screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast in both themes, responsive controls and actionable loading, empty, error and success states.
